Step 1

Combined score

Chat and audio both count and agreement is rewarded. Crossing 2.2 makes one candidate per spike. Chat peaks shift back 12 s. Quiet for 5 min? Judge the best window anyway.

→
Step 2

AI judge

Score 0-100, reason, hook-first start/end (20-55 s), title, caption, safe flag. Below 55 or unsafe: not cut. No judge available: deterministic fallback score.
